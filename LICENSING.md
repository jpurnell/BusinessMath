# Licensing — BusinessMath

**Short version:** free if you publish your source, paid if you do not.

---

## Open source — AGPLv3

BusinessMath is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3**
(see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).

You may use, modify and distribute it freely. In return, AGPLv3 asks that if you
distribute the software **or make it available to users over a network**, you
release the complete corresponding source of your work under the same licence.

That network clause — AGPLv3 §13 — is the difference between this licence and
plain GPLv3, and it is deliberate: running this as a hosted service is a form of
use we intend the copyleft to reach.

**Use it under AGPLv3 if you are:** an open-source project, a researcher, a
student, or anyone content to publish the source of what you build.

---

## Commercial licence

If you want to embed BusinessMath in a **proprietary product**, or offer it as part of a
**hosted service**, without the obligation to publish your own source, a
commercial licence removes the copyleft terms.

That is the intended route for banks, funds, corporates and software vendors
whose policy or commercial position makes AGPL unworkable — which is a normal
and expected position, not an obstacle.

**Contact:** open an issue titled `Commercial licence enquiry`, or reach the
maintainer through the address on the GitHub profile.

---

## What is *not* restricted

To be unambiguous, none of the following requires a commercial licence:

- reading the source, or learning from it
- running it privately, inside your own organisation, without distributing it or
  exposing it over a network to others
- building and publishing an open-source project on top of it
- contributing to it

---

## Dependencies

The permissive layers of this family — **SwiftExcelCore**, **SwiftXLSX** and
**SwiftZIP** — are Apache 2.0, deliberately. They are shared vocabulary and file
handling; they are not the licensed value, and they carry no copyleft.

Copyleft may depend on permissive but never the reverse, so this arrangement is
stable rather than accidental.

## Contributions

By contributing you agree that your contribution may be distributed under both
AGPLv3 and a commercial licence. Dual licensing requires the project to hold all
the rights, which is why this term exists; it is not a transfer of your
copyright.
